## Not all situations are the same

A musician doesn't have the same needs as a cultural collective.
A neighbourhood business doesn't work the same as an association.

We build custom software, but we start from a base we have consolidated through experience, and we adapt it to each project: everyone's needs, and also the imaginative challenges we enjoy meeting.

We don't offer a catalogue. We offer solutions designed for concrete situations.

## Usual situation vs. what we propose

| Profile | The usual situation | What we propose |
|---|---|---|
| [Musicians and artists](/solucions/musics/) | A template-looking website that doesn't reflect who you are. Content scattered across platforms you don't control. An out-of-date calendar. Every change means calling someone. | A site designed from scratch, agenda connected to what you already use, discography and press kit in one place, and publishing you can do yourself. |
| [Collectives, associations and cultural spaces](/solucions/collectius/) | Communication depends on tools nobody decided to use. Documents live on corporate platforms. No member can manage the site without technical help. | A platform that not only informs but also helps you manage: content, members, documents and privacy by design. |
| [Micro-businesses and freelancers](/solucions/microempreses/) | A site made years ago that nobody dares touch. Subscriptions nobody remembers signing up for. No control over what visitors' data is used for. | A custom site with shop or bookings if needed, own email and domain, and a system the owner can maintain without depending on anyone. |
