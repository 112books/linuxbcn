## What a band website includes

The base we use in every music project:

1. **A gig list that updates itself.** Gigs are published on Bandsintown or an iCal calendar, as you already do, and appear on the website with the ticket link.
2. **A page for every record.** Each record has its own URL, cover, credits and links to streaming platforms, so every record can be found in search engines.
3. **A booking page with EPK.** Formats, line-up, guest artists and a downloadable press kit for venues, festivals and media.
4. **Synced videos.** Your YouTube channels show up automatically, with nothing uploaded twice.
5. **Photos and reviews.** Albums and concert reviews linked from where they already live, with no heavy files duplicated.
6. **Members and history.** A profile for each musician and a timeline of the band's milestones.
7. **Several languages.** Catalan, Spanish, English or whatever you need, each with its own URL.
8. **A simple editor.** Texts, biography and press are changed without touching code.

And underneath, what you don't see but matters: a fast, accessible website (WCAG 2.1 AA), structured data for search engines and AI engines, and no plugins to update.

If you first want the list of what matters and what to skip, you have it in [what a band's website needs to cover](/guies/web-per-a-una-banda/): written so you can use it with us or without us.
