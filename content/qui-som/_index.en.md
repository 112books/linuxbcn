## LinuxBCN

Technology consultancy specialising in digital identity, web development and custom applications.

We work with free software. We host data with European providers, preferably Catalan ones. We design systems that clients can understand, maintain and control.

Since 2001, with no dependency on Google, Meta or the major technology platforms.

Behind LinuxBCN is Joan «Linux» Martínez, founder and project coordinator, working with a team of specialist collaborators in design, content, infrastructure and digital communication.

---

## Track record

Twenty-five years supporting cultural, artistic and social projects in building coherent, sustainable digital presences. These are the works that best explain what we do.

**Migrations and archives**
- [**Blog de Pocallum**](/projectes/blog-pocallum/) — 2,353 posts and 137,000 words, from WordPress to Hugo, with 2,360 verified URLs
- [**9 Barris Imatge**](/projectes/migracio-blogger-9barrisimatge/) — 3,006 posts since 2008, from Blogger to a system of their own, with zero errors
- [**Barcelona Big Blues Band**](/projectes/barcelona-big-blues-band/) and [**Ivan Kovačević**](/projectes/ivan-kovacevic/) — multilingual musician sites, from WordPress to Hugo

**Applications**
- [**Taro Photo App**](/projectes/taro-photo-app/) — members, contests and publishing for photography collectives (AGPL-3.0)
- [**Public voting**](/projectes/votacio-public/) — anonymous, in-room voting for exhibitions
- [**Konsento**](/projectes/konsento/) — assembly decisions for community spaces
- [**Cuida**](/projectes/cuida/) — home care coordination
- [**GoatCounter Dashboard**](/projectes/goatcounter-dashboard/) — cookie-free analytics
- [**Positivador**](/projectes/app-positivador/) — in-browser darkroom tool

**Websites and platforms**
- [**112 Books**](/projectes/112books/) — independent photobook publisher
- [**Nau Bostik**](/projectes/nau-bostik/) — self-managed sociocultural space, La Sagrera
- [**FAVB**](/projectes/favb/) and [**Revista Carrer**](/projectes/carrer/) — Barcelona neighbourhood media
- [**Bratia**](/projectes/bratia/) — Balkan Gypsy Jazz band
- [**Llumàtics**](/projectes/llumatics/) — analogue and experimental photography
- [**Machiroku**](/projectes/machiroku/) — artisan Japanese cuisine
- [**40 years of blues**](/projectes/40anysblues/) — archive of the Barcelona blues scene
- [**Ressona**](/projectes/ressona/) — digital identity platform for artists

And a dozen more musicians, artists, collectives and small businesses.

Every site we build carries [**Powered by LinuxBCN**](/powered-by-linuxbcn/) in the footer. Not for branding — because we stand behind what we make.
