## What happens when you write

You get a **person**, not a system. Within 24 hours (working days) you receive a first answer: whether I can help, how, and if I am not the right person, who you can turn to.

There is no sales script and no commercial calls. The first conversation is to understand the problem, not to close anything.

## What we would like to know

- Who you are and what you do (or what the project does).
- What you have now: website, tools, social media, who maintains it.
- What bothers you or what is not working.
- Whether there are deadlines.

With that we can already tell you whether what you need is small, large, or whether the best move is to change nothing.

## What we do not do

- Advertising or social media management.
- Complex shops with stock, variants and shipping (we will point you to people who do).
- Maintenance of servers we have no access to.
- Anything that depends on tracking people.

## If what you want is something small

That too. A domain change, a one-hour training session, an accessibility review or a second opinion on a quote. Not everything has to be a big project: write anyway and we will look at it.
