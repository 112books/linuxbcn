## A publisher that accompanies authors

112Books is an independent publisher in Barcelona specialising in **photobooks and photographic projects**. It does more than print: it accompanies photographers and authors through the whole process — from the first idea to the finished work — with layout, image selection and sequencing, printing, print runs and distribution.

It is a LinuxBCN project of our own, which is also why we use it as a test bed: if we would not trust a decision for a client, we do not make it here either.

## The problem was not visual, it was structural

Over the years the site had grown by accumulation: a base theme modified with layers of CSS, plugins covering what the theme did not do, and sections with no coherence between them.

The result was a site that *worked* but did not explain who 112Books is. A new visitor could not tell whether they were looking at a printer, a gallery, a bookshop or a publisher.

## What we did

In **2026** we rebuilt the site from the first commit with a **WordPress FSE theme built from scratch** (block templates, a design-tokens file and clean CSS). The goal was to organise the information around what the publisher actually does:

- **The accompaniment**: the core of the business, from idea to book.
- **The catalogue**: available photobooks, with full detail and an integrated shop.
- **The authors directory**: who has worked with the publisher and wants to appear.

Plus seven service areas (photobooks, promotion, exhibitions, prints, postcards, framing and photographic catalogue) explained without endless catalogues.

## The result

The homepage explains in five seconds what 112Books does and leads to the two things that matter: hiring the accompaniment or buying a book. Catalogue and basket share the same visual identity as the rest of the site, because **WooCommerce was integrated from the design, not added afterwards**.

The detail of the theme is in [the project page](/projectes/112books.eu-theme/).

## Technology

WordPress with its own FSE theme, integrated WooCommerce, no unnecessary plugins (own form and lightbox) and all code versioned on GitHub from the first commit.
