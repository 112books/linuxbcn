## A federation with decades of history

The **FAVB** —Federation of Neighbourhood Associations of Barcelona— is one of the oldest and most representative neighbourhood organisations in the city. It brings together neighbourhood associations, coordinates campaigns and publishes its own content, such as [Carrer.cat](/projectes/carrer/) magazine.

For an organisation like this the website is not a business card: it is infrastructure. Publications, announcements, documents and campaigns that the city reads pass through it.

## The problem: a site that grew by accumulation

When a site has been running for years and the people publishing keep changing, the same thing always happens: sections nobody remembers the purpose of, duplicated files, a homepage that no longer explains what the organisation does, and a team afraid to touch anything in case it breaks.

The challenge was not to build a new site. It was to **keep what already worked** — the archive, the publications, the campaigns — and make it easier to maintain without losing anything along the way.

## What we did

- **Content architecture** organised around what the federation actually does: who we are, what we do, publications, campaigns and services for associations.
- **Frictionless publishing** for a non-technical team: publishing without touching code and without fear.
- **Maintenance with backups** before every change, plus planned updates.
- **Accessibility and performance** reviewed on the main pages.
- **Connection with Carrer magazine**, which has its own site and shares the same voice.

## Why we did not migrate this one

Not every site should leave WordPress. At the FAVB several people publish, campaigns change often and the editorial structure is consolidated: here the database-backed CMS does the job, and removing it would make things worse.

What was needed was not a change of technology but **tidying it up, documenting it and maintaining it**. We say so when it applies: if your WordPress works and the problem is order, not platform, we will not sell you a migration.

## Technology

WordPress with a theme run with judgement, own hosting, HTTPS and backups. The goal is that the site costs the team who uses it as little unnecessary work as possible.
