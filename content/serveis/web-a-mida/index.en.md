## What "custom" means

It means the decisions come from the project: which pages are needed, how information is organised, how it reads on a phone and what the person editing it must be able to do. It does not mean "expensive" or "with more features". It often means **fewer** things, done properly.

Most sites we see have the opposite problem: a template with twenty sections nobody fills, widgets that load for no known reason and an admin panel nobody understands. We start from a minimal base and add only what the project asks for.

## Who we work with

- **Artists, musicians and bands** who want their own site instead of depending on a profile on a platform.
- **Cultural spaces and collectives** that need to inform and, often, to manage things.
- **Small businesses and professionals** who want a presence consistent with how they work.
- **Projects coming from a WordPress** they can no longer maintain: in that case, start with [how we move a WordPress site to static](/serveis/migracio-wordpress/).

## What is included

- Custom design, with your identity or built from scratch.
- A content structure designed to grow without a rebuild.
- **Visual editor** so the team publishes without code.
- Multilingual (Catalan and English at least) when the project needs it.
- Optimised images, semantic HTML, WCAG AA accessibility.
- Forms that reach your inbox without intermediary services.
- Cookie-free analytics, if you want it.
- Training and written notes.

## What is not included

- **Complex shops.** Small catalogues yes; stock, variants, coupons and shipping, no.
- **Member areas or closed courses.** If the project needs it, we say so and look for the option that fits, even if it is not ours.
- **Marketing.** We do not sell advertising or social media management.

## How we do it

### 1. Listen

First we understand what the site must do: who arrives, what they look for and what should happen next. Without that, any design is decoration.

### 2. Structure

We define pages, hierarchy and content before touching the design. This is the part that decides whether the site will be easy to maintain three years from now.

### 3. Build

We design and code with Hugo, optimised images, semantic HTML and WCAG AA accessibility. Every choice has a reason and can be explained.

### 4. Support

Training, written notes and an accompanied launch. The site must end up in the hands of the people who use it, not ours.

## Technology

Hugo generates the site; the result is static files that do not depend on a database or a PHP server. For whoever maintains it, that means fewer things that can break. For whoever visits it, less waiting.
