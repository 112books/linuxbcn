Every project starts by understanding what it really needs, not by picking a template. These are the jobs we do most often; if yours is not here, ask anyway.

The most common entry point is **leaving WordPress**: a site that publishes content but demands too much maintenance. If that is you, start with the migration pillar.

## How we approach a job

We do not sell fixed packages. After a first conversation we know whether you need a website, a migration or an application — and also what you do **not** need to do. Often the answer is smaller than the client expected, and that is part of the work too.

Budgets run by the hour: you pay for work done, with a record of what was done. No monthly fees for things that are already solved.

## What every service has in common

- **Free software** whenever there is an equivalent alternative.
- **Your visitors' data** is not sold or sent to third parties.
- **You end up understanding it**: training and written notes, not dependency.
- **Accessibility and legal compliance** as part of the work, not as an extra.
