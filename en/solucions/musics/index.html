Solutions for artists and musicians

Autonomous, clear and sustainable digital presence

The usual situation

You have a website, social media, content — but no system.

Concerts not updated. Biography different on every platform. Videos on YouTube, photos on Instagram, music on Spotify, and the website connects none of it.

Any change requires touching five different places. Or depending on someone else to do it.

What we propose

A digital presence designed as a system.

A website that connects what already exists — concerts, music, videos, photographs — without duplicating content or creating new dependencies.

You manage what you want to manage. The rest runs by itself.

We don’t start from zero with every band: the Barcelona Big Blues Band website was built on the same base as Ivan Kovačević’s. Less repeated work means fewer errors, a professional result from day one and a project that emerging bands can afford too.

What a band website includes

The base we use in every music project:

  1. A gig list that updates itself. Gigs are published on Bandsintown or an iCal calendar, as you already do, and appear on the website with the ticket link.
  2. A page for every record. Each record has its own URL, cover, credits and links to streaming platforms, so every record can be found in search engines.
  3. A booking page with EPK. Formats, line-up, guest artists and a downloadable press kit for venues, festivals and media.
  4. Synced videos. Your YouTube channels show up automatically, with nothing uploaded twice.
  5. Photos and reviews. Albums and concert reviews linked from where they already live, with no heavy files duplicated.
  6. Members and history. A profile for each musician and a timeline of the band’s milestones.
  7. Several languages. Catalan, Spanish, English or whatever you need, each with its own URL.
  8. A simple editor. Texts, biography and press are changed without touching code.

And underneath, what you don’t see but matters: a fast, accessible website (WCAG 2.1 AA), structured data for search engines and AI engines, and no plugins to update.

If you first want the list of what matters and what to skip, you have it in what a band’s website needs to cover: written so you can use it with us or without us.

And if the project needs it

  • Newsletter to keep your audience informed without depending on social media
  • Contact form for promoters and media
  • Embedded player and direct sales through Bandcamp
  • Technical rider for venues and sound crews, public or private
  • Merchandise, a tour map or a members-only area for fans

Always with the same criteria: less dispersion, more control.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a band website take?

It mostly depends on the content you already have: records, biography, photos, videos. Because we start from a proven base, the work goes into your music and your image, not into building the structure from scratch. Before we start, you get a concrete timeline.

Can we update it ourselves?

Yes. Biography, line-up, press and texts are edited from a simple content manager, with no code. And the things that change most, gigs and videos, don’t need touching at all: they update themselves.

What happens to our Bandsintown, YouTube or calendar?

You keep using them as you do now. The website reads your gigs from Bandsintown or an iCal calendar and the videos from your YouTube channels, and shows them automatically. No habits change.

We're on WordPress. What do we lose if we leave?

Not your content: discography, biography, news and images are extracted from WordPress and migrated automatically, with nothing copied by hand. What you lose is the plugins, the constant security updates and a heavy admin panel nobody wants to open.

Can you also help with our image, press kit and communication?

Yes, through Ressona, the digital identity platform for artists we run with Pocallum. LinuxBCN handles the technical side (website, migration, automation) and Ressona the identity: visuals, press kit and communication.

Do you only work with established bands?

No. We work with soloists, emerging bands and groups with long careers. Reusing a base that already works is what makes a professional website affordable for a band that is just starting out too.
