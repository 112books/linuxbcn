Most digital problems
aren’t technical

They’re about structure.

You have a website you can’t update because it depends on an agency that takes three weeks to reply. Your Instagram has 2,000 followers but your domain expires next month. You use big-tech tools because everyone does, but your communications sit on servers you don’t control and can’t audit. Your contact form sends data to an American server you’ve never heard of.

Often, the problem isn’t that you lack technology. It’s that you have too much technology you don’t understand, don’t control, and couldn’t change even if you wanted to.

What we do

We accompany artists, cultural collectives and small businesses in building a coherent, free and sustainable digital presence.

Concretely:

  • Websites

    That you can edit yourself, that don't depend on any platform, that don't expose your visitors' data to third parties.

  • Domains and email

    Your own, properly configured, with full control. No intermediaries who might change the terms overnight.

  • Servers and hosting

    With European providers, preferably Catalan. Data on our continent, under legislation that protects you.

  • Communication tools

    Forms, newsletters, analytics — without Google, without Meta, without hidden trackers on your site.

  • Training and support

    So you can maintain the system yourself. Minimal dependence on us is part of the service.

  • Honest analytics and intelligent interpretation

    We use GoatCounter to analyse visitor behaviour without compromising their anonymity. Real data, no trackers — so we can assess what we offer and how we offer it.

  • GDPR audit and compliance

    We audit the implementation of data protection laws on your digital project and help you meet all current legal requirements.

We don’t make catalogue websites. We don’t apply templates. We don’t recommend trendy technology.

We listen, understand the situation, and design the system that makes sense for each project.

Free software isn’t ideology.
It’s practice.

When you use free tools, the code is public. Someone has checked whether it does what it says it does. Someone has verified it doesn’t send your data to places you haven’t authorised.

When you use closed tools, you have to trust. And trust is expensive when what’s at stake is your relationship with your audience, your internal communications, or your clients’ data.

We have seen cultural projects lose years of work because a platform changed its terms overnight. We have seen musicians without access to their own fan base because it lived in an account they didn’t control. We have seen collectives paying indefinitely for tools they didn’t need but didn’t know how to remove.

Digital sovereignty isn’t a luxury. It’s basic hygiene.

How we do it

LinuxBCN has existed since 2001. Twenty-five years accompanying musicians, cultural spaces, independent publishers, neighbourhood collectives and values-driven small businesses. Each project is different. The approach is the same:

  1. 01
    We listen

    We understand where the project comes from and where it wants to go. No assumptions, no pre-cooked solutions.

  2. 02
    Diagnosis

    We assess what exists, what's needed, and what's surplus. The minimum system that solves the real problem.

  3. 03
    Build

    We design and implement with free software and tools the client can maintain without us.

  4. 04
    Support

    Training and support until the system is truly theirs. Dependence on us must be minimal.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does LinuxBCN do?

We help artists, cultural collectives and small businesses build and maintain their digital presence: websites, custom applications, migrations and their own infrastructure. We don’t apply templates: we design the system that fits each project and hand it over to the people who will use it.

Who do you work with?

Musicians and artists, cultural collectives and venues, independent publishers, neighbourhood media and values-driven small businesses. If your project doesn’t fit any of these exactly, let’s talk anyway.

Why free software instead of a closed platform?

So the code is auditable and the data is yours. With free software you can know what the system does, change it and take it with you if you ever need to; with a closed platform you have to trust it won’t change the terms or use your data without permission.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Projects include a simple content manager to edit texts, images and pages without touching code. If you’d rather we handle maintenance, that’s an option too.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A simple website can be live in a few weeks; a migration or a custom application takes longer. Before starting we run a diagnosis and agree on realistic deadlines.

What happens once the website is live?

We offer ongoing support and maintenance by the hour. The system is yours and you can maintain it wherever you like, with no mandatory fees or forced dependency.

Where are the website and data hosted?

On your own server or with a European provider, whichever suits you. We prioritise data sovereignty and avoid services that send data to third countries without guarantees.
