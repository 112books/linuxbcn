Who we are

LinuxBCN

Technology consultancy specialising in digital identity, web development and custom applications.

We work with free software. We host data with European providers, preferably Catalan ones. We design systems that clients can understand, maintain and control.

Since 2001, with no dependency on Google, Meta or the major technology platforms.

Behind LinuxBCN is Joan «Linux» Martínez, founder and project coordinator, working with a team of specialist collaborators in design, content, infrastructure and digital communication.

Track record

Twenty-five years supporting cultural, artistic and social projects in building coherent, sustainable digital presences.

We have developed and maintain websites and applications for:

  • Bratia — independent rock band website
  • Nau Bostik — self-managed sociocultural space, La Sagrera
  • Llumàtics — analogue photography school
  • 112 Books — independent photobook publisher
  • FAVB — Federation of Barcelona Neighbourhood Associations
  • Revista Carrer — FAVB’s publication
  • 9 Barris Acull — support organisation for migrants
  • Restaurant Machiroku — artisan Japanese cuisine
  • Family Art Tattoo — tattoo studio
  • Ressona.eu — digital identity platform for emerging artists
  • App Positivador — analogue darkroom web application
  • And a dozen more musicians, artists and collectives.

Every site we build carries Powered by LinuxBCN in the footer. Not for branding — because we stand behind what we make.

How we work

Every project starts with the same question: What is the minimum system that will solve the real problem?

  1. 01
    We listen

    We understand where the project comes from and where it wants to go. No assumptions, no pre-cooked solutions.

  2. 02
    Diagnosis

    We assess what exists, what's needed, and what's surplus. The minimum system that solves the real problem.

  3. 03
    Build

    We design and implement with free software and tools the client can maintain without us.

  4. 04
    Support

    Training and support until the system is truly theirs. Dependence on us must be minimal.

Convictions we don’t negotiate

  • No Google

    No Analytics, no Fonts, no Maps, no Tag Manager. Google is the business model that normalised mass surveillance as a service.

  • No Kit Digital

    Spain's digital subsidy scheme concentrates public resources in large tech integrators without the beneficiary gaining real sovereignty.

  • No artificial dependencies

    We don't take on projects that require what we won't do. And we don't build systems that chain clients to us indefinitely.

  • Data in Europe

    European providers, preferably Catalan. Our clients' data stays on the European continent, under legislation that protects them.

Who we work with

  • Artists and musicians

    Your own presence, scheduling and promotion. No templates, no intermediaries controlling your communication.

  • Entities and cultural collectives

    Free tools, digital sovereignty and internal management without dependency on big platforms.

  • Values-driven businesses

    A digital presence coherent with what you do, systems you genuinely own and don't depend on external subscriptions.

If your project doesn’t fit neatly into any of these profiles, get in touch anyway. Every situation is different.
