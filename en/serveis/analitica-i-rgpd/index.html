Analytics and GDPR

Measure without spying, comply without useless paperwork

Cookie-free, tracker-free web analytics, plus data protection and accessibility audits. Know what happens on your site without selling your visitors.

Analytics that do not spy

Most analytics tools do not measure the site: they build a profile of every visitor and sell it or keep it for other uses. We do the opposite: we count visits, pages and sources without identifying people, with our own counter on the same domain that cannot be blocked and needs no cookies.

What we measure and what we do not

Yes: visits, most-viewed pages, language, device, aggregated source and conversions (for example, how many people submit the form).

No: personal data, individual journeys, profiles, cross-site tracking or persistent identifiers. There is nothing to sell because there is nothing to exploit.

GDPR: what we review

  • Which data the site really collects (not what the legal text says: what the code collects).
  • Who receives it: forms, hosting, analytics, email.
  • How long it is kept and how it is deleted.
  • Legal basis for each processing activity and clear information in the privacy policy.
  • Processors: contracts and guarantees where external services are involved.
  • Incident procedure and how data subject rights are handled.

Accessibility

A site that cannot be used with a keyboard or a screen reader is not just a breach: it is a closed door. We review contrast, visible focus, forms, heading structure, alternative text and navigation, and we fix it.

How we do it

1. Decide what needs to be known

Before measuring anything, we decide which three or four questions need an answer. If a piece of data changes no decision, it is not collected.

2. Set up measurement

We install cookie-free analytics with our own counter, so visitors do not have to decide anything or accept any banner.

3. Review compliance

We go through legal texts, forms, processors, data retention and actual cookies, and we write down what is done and why.

4. Leave it documented

Record of processing activities, legitimate interest assessment and an incident procedure. Compliance is documentation, not a ticked box.

If what needs reviewing is the technical base —speed, accessibility, structure— we cover that in custom websites.

An honest warning

This work will not make you sell more on its own. It will help you understand what happens on your site with data you do not have to take on faith, and it will remove a legal risk from your shoulders. If you are after perfect advertising attribution, that is not our service and we will say so.

Frequently asked questions

Can I have statistics without a cookie banner?

Yes, if the analytics installs no cookies and does not identify people. With our own counter and aggregated data, no consent is needed to measure visits, but you must inform users in the privacy policy and limit what is collected.

Will I lose information compared to Google Analytics?

You will lose what you do not need: advertising attribution, visitor profiles and cross-site tracking. You will gain data you understand, that does not change criteria every month and that does not expose visitors to third parties.

What exactly is a GDPR audit?

Reviewing which data the site collects, who receives it, how long it is kept and on what legal basis. It results in legal texts matching reality, forms that collect less and a record of processing activities you can show if asked.

Does accessibility fall under this too?

Yes. An inaccessible site is a legal problem and, above all, a real problem for the people using it. We review WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, keyboard, focus, forms, semantic structure.

Can you audit a site you did not build?

Yes. We look at what is there, whoever built it. If code changes are needed, we describe them in detail with a separate quote.
