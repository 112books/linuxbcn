Barcelona Big Blues Band

2026 · rhythm & blues big band

Website for the Barcelona Big Blues Band, migrated from WordPress to Hugo: lightweight, trilingual, with automatic gig listings, discography and booking kit.

Europe’s largest rhythm & blues big band

The Barcelona Big Blues Band is an ensemble of up to fifteen musicians led by double bassist Ivan Kovačević, based at Barcelona’s Jamboree Jazz Club for more than ten years. It plays blues, swing and rhythm & blues in big-band format and backs guest artists such as Dani Nel·lo, Ray Gelato, Mike Sanchez and Myriam Swanson.

The previous site was a WordPress install: plugins for everything, even for the cookie banner, and a structure that didn’t match what a band needs to show.

The problem

A big band speaks to two audiences at once: people who want to go to the next gig and people who want to book it. The first need an up-to-date agenda. The second need line-up, repertoire, guests, videos and a press kit without having to ask for it by email.

If keeping all that current means logging into a heavy back end, the site goes stale and stops selling gigs.

The solution

A static Hugo site, built on the same base as Ivan Kovačević’s website. Reusing the structure means less work, fewer errors and a professional result from day one.

A gig list that updates itself Gigs are published on Bandsintown, as the band already did, and show up on the site with ticket links and an add-to-calendar option.

Complete discography Twelve records, each with its own page and guest artist. Every record has its own URL, which makes it findable in search engines.

Booking page for promoters A page designed for venues and festivals: configurable format (from combo to full big band), full line-up, guest artists and electronic press kit (EPK).

Photos and gig reports The photo section links to the concert reports published on blog.pocallum.cat. The site doesn’t duplicate files: it points to where they already live.

Editable without code Biography, line-up, press and texts can be changed through a simple content manager (Decap CMS).

Trilingual Catalan, Spanish and English, each with its own URL and correct hreflang.

The migration

The WordPress content was extracted through its API, converted to Markdown, and the original images were downloaded. Nothing was copied by hand, and the old site could be retired without losing discography, biography or history.

Result

A lightweight, fast site that practically runs itself: the band publishes gigs where it always has and the site does the rest.

WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, Schema.org structured data, installable web app (PWA) and llms.txt for AI search engines.

Technology

Hugo · Decap CMS · Bandsintown · Multilingual (CA/ES/EN) · WCAG 2.1 AA · Schema.org · PWA

What we solved

How do you make a band's gig list update itself?

Gigs are published on Bandsintown, as the band already did, and the site shows them automatically with tickets and an add-to-calendar option.

What does a venue or festival need to book a big band?

A booking page with format, line-up, guest artists and a downloadable press kit, without having to ask for it.

Yes. The content was extracted through the API, converted to Markdown and the original images were kept.

A professional, lightweight, trilingual site that practically runs itself, so the band can focus on playing.
