Ivan Kovačević

2026 · musician

Website for double bassist Ivan Kovačević, migrated from WordPress to Hugo: four languages, one agenda for all his projects and a discography of 50+ records.

One musician, many projects, one website

Ivan Kovačević is a Serbian double bassist based in Barcelona since 2001. He has played with Los Mambo Jambo, The Nu Niles and Down Home, is a founding member of the Barcelona Gipsy balKan Orchestra, leads the Barcelona Big Blues Band and fronts Bratia. He has played on more than fifty records.

His website had gone through iWeb and then WordPress. Neither solved the real problem: how to present a career spread across many bands without the site turning into a messy drawer.

The problem

A musician who plays in several bands has gigs on each band’s calendar, videos on several channels and records on different labels. Keeping all of that up to date by hand on one site is unworkable: either the work gets duplicated or the site falls behind.

The solution

A static Hugo site that aggregates what already exists instead of asking for it to be written again.

One agenda The calendars of all his projects are merged into a single agenda. Each gig shows which band it is with, carries MusicEvent structured data and can be subscribed to as a calendar.

Videos from every channel Videos from several YouTube channels are aggregated automatically and tagged by the project they come from.

A discography of 50+ records Each record has its own page and URL, grouped by project: Los Mambo Jambo, Barcelona Gipsy balKan Orchestra, The Nu Niles, Lazy Jumpers, Bernat Font Trio, 5 Spots and Barcelona Big Blues Band.

Nine projects, nine pages Each band has its own page with history and links, and the site links to Bratia’s and the Big Blues Band’s own websites.

Four languages English, Spanish, Catalan and Serbian. The original WordPress content (English and Spanish) was migrated automatically; Catalan and Serbian were translated separately.

Own contact and booking A booking form handled on the server, with no external services, plus an on-site search.

The migration

The content was extracted from WordPress through its API, converted to Markdown, and the original images were downloaded. The same technical base was later reused for the Barcelona Big Blues Band website.

Result

A single address that brings together a musician’s whole career and updates itself with the work he already does: each band publishes its gigs and videos, and the site collects them.

WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, Schema.org structured data and installable web app (PWA).

Technology

Hugo · iCal aggregation · YouTube · Multilingual (EN/ES/CA/SR) · WCAG 2.1 AA · Schema.org · PWA

What we solved

How do you bring a musician who plays in many bands together on one site?

By aggregating each project's calendars and video channels in one place, without duplicating work.

Can a site run in four languages without making maintenance harder?

Yes. Hugo handles English, Spanish, Catalan and Serbian with their own URLs and hreflang, and the original WordPress content was migrated automatically.

How do you make a discography of 50+ records findable?

By giving each record its own page and URL, grouped by project.

What is the end benefit?

A single address that brings together a whole career and updates itself with the work the musician already does.
