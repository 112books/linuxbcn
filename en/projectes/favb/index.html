FAVB

2024 · neighbourhood federation

Years administering the Federation of Neighbourhood Associations of Barcelona's website: redesign, separation of Carrer magazine and ongoing maintenance.

A federation with decades of history

The FAVB —Federation of Neighbourhood Associations of Barcelona— is one of the oldest and most representative neighbourhood organisations in the city. It brings together neighbourhood associations, coordinates campaigns and publishes its own content.

For an organisation like this the website is not a business card: it is infrastructure. Publications, announcements, documents and campaigns that the city reads pass through it.

Years administering this website

We have been administering the FAVB website for years. In that time it has been through many phases: template changes, new sections, moves, emergency patches and clean-ups. This is the kind of site that is not built in a day: it is maintained.

That continuity is half the value. An organisation’s site is not judged on launch day but on how it looks five years later: whether the team still knows how to publish, whether the archive can still be found and whether anyone had to rebuild everything.

The latest phase: redesign and splitting off Carrer

In the latest phase we redesigned the site and split Carrer magazine into its own website, with a design built from scratch and adapted to the publication’s image.

They were two different things asking for different rhythms and structures: a federation that coordinates, informs and serves its associations, and a magazine that publishes neighbourhood journalism. Mixing them forced compromises that suited neither.

What is still pending

At the FAVB we still have to redesign the site’s theme, in the not too distant future. It is not urgent, but it matters: when we do it, it will follow the same criteria as everything else — without breaking the archive, without losing what works and leaving it easy for the team to maintain.

Why we do not migrate this one

Not every site should leave WordPress. At the FAVB several people publish, campaigns change often and the editorial structure is consolidated: here the database-backed CMS does the job, and removing it would make things worse.

What is needed is not a change of technology but tidying it up, documenting it and maintaining it. We say so when it applies: if your WordPress works and the problem is order, not platform, we will not sell you a migration.

Technology

WordPress with a theme that is the heir of many phases, own hosting, HTTPS and backups. The next step is already decided: rebuild the theme and leave it clean.
