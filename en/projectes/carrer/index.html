Carrer.cat

2024 · neighbourhood publication

Website for Carrer.cat, the magazine of Barcelona's neighbourhood movement: complete PDF archive, article publishing and a hybrid print-and-web model.

The neighbourhood movement’s magazine, online

Carrer is the publication of the FAVB —Federation of Neighbourhood Associations of Barcelona— with decades of work defending neighbourhood rights. It is a magazine made by people from the neighbourhoods, describing the city from the ground up: housing, mobility, public space, social rights.

The challenge was to bring a consolidated print publication online without losing its editorial essence.

A website of its own, separated from the federation

The magazine used to live inside the FAVB website. We split it off because they are two different things: a federation that coordinates, informs and serves its associations, and a publication with its own rhythm, structure and image.

The design was built from scratch, adapted to the printed magazine’s image — not a template, not a section of the larger site — so that Carrer readers recognise the same publication when they arrive from their phone.

Two problems in one

Simply posting PDF issues was not enough. Two things had to be solved at once:

  1. Memory: decades of neighbourhood journalism that existed only on paper and that nobody could consult.
  2. Currency: what happens in the neighbourhood this week, which cannot wait for the next printed issue.

The solution: a hybrid model

Complete digital archive. Every issue of the magazine in PDF, accessible, organised and searchable by date and content. Decades of neighbourhood journalism, available online.

Web article publishing. Beyond the archive, the system allows publishing standalone articles when the content calls for it — without waiting for the printed issue — with the same typographic dignity as paper.

A structure built for the people making it. The magazine is maintained by people who write and edit, not by systems people: publishing a new article should not involve three screens or asking anyone for permission.

What the publication gains

What used to exist only in the newspaper library can now be found in a search. Old issues can be linked and cited. And current affairs no longer depend on the printing calendar.

At the same time, paper continues to exist: the website does not replace the magazine, it extends it.

Technology

WordPress with a custom content structure, own hosting, HTTPS and backups. Here the CMS does the job because there is an editorial team publishing often; what was needed was not a change of platform but ordering the content and making it findable.
