Carrer.cat

2024 · neighbourhood publication

Website for Carrer.cat, the reference publication of Barcelona's neighbourhood movement. Digital communication for the magazine. LinuxBCN.

The Catalan neighbourhood movement’s magazine online

Carrer is the publication of FAVB — the Federation of Neighbourhood Associations of Barcelona — with decades of experience defending neighbourhood rights.

The challenge was to bring a consolidated print publication to the internet without losing its editorial essence.

The solution

We built the project from scratch with two clear axes:

Complete digital archive All magazine issues in PDF, accessible, organised and searchable. Decades of neighbourhood journalism, available online.

Web article publishing Beyond the archive, the system allows publishing independent articles when the content calls for it — without waiting for the next print issue.

A hybrid model that respects the publication’s tradition and adapts it to the possibilities of digital.

What we solved

How do you take a neighbourhood publication online?

Website and digital communication for the magazine, with manageable content.

Can you publish with no dependencies?

Yes, with free software and your own hosting.

What is Carrer.cat?

It is the digital version of the magazine published in print by the Federation of Barcelona Neighbourhood Associations.
