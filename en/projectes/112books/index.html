Website for 112Books, independent analogue photography publisher. Digital identity and online presence with free software. LinuxBCN.

An independent photography publisher

112Books is an independent publisher focused on photobooks and photographic projects.

The challenge was not just publishing content. It was structuring a coherent and sustainable digital editorial system.

Website currently under renovation — the new version will include a news blog and an integrated shop, built with Hugo to reduce weight and improve performance.

→ 112books.eu

What we solved

How do you give a photography publisher its own digital presence?

A website of its own with a structured catalogue and digital identity, without relying on third-party platforms.

Do you lose control of the data?

No: hosting and analytics are our own, with no cookies or external services.

What does an independent publisher gain?

Its own, sovereign digital presence to showcase its catalogue and connect with readers, without relying on third-party platforms.
